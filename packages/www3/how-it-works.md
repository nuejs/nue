## Spin world-class sites without a design/engineering team
Think about the sites you admire. Apple. Linear. Stripe. A proper design system makes this level of polish available for everyone.

[montage of sites with links to them]

It's like your Figma project, but better: a complete system expressed in code. Typography, color, spacing, interactions, responsivity, motion design. All layers covered, directly accessible for designers, engineers, and AI. No designer-developer handoff. No translation errors.

You build this foundation once. Then every new site inherits it automatically. Marketing site, documentation, blog, store. Add overrides for personality and the system ensures quality.

This is how you spin world-class sites without a design/engineering team.
